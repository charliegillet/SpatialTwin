# ADA reference (paraphrased)

Source: U.S. Department of Justice, *2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design*.
These are paraphrases, not quotations. Cite the section number.

- **§404.2.3 Clear width (doors and doorways).** A door opening needs at least
  32 in (815 mm) of clear width, measured with a swinging door open 90 degrees,
  between the door face and the opposite stop. When the doorway is deeper than
  24 in (610 mm), the opening needs at least 36 in (915 mm).
- **§403.5.1 Clear width (walking surfaces).** An accessible route needs at
  least 36 in (915 mm) of clear width. It may narrow to 32 in (815 mm) for no more
  than 24 in (610 mm) of length, with such narrow segments at least 48 in
  (1220 mm) apart.
- **§304.3.1 Circular turning space.** A turning space can be a circle at least
  60 in (1525 mm) in diameter.

## How to use

- Compare the **estimated range** (low to high) with the minimum. Say "meets ADA
  §404.2.3 within the model's estimated range (model-reported)" only if the low
  end meets it. Say "within measured range" only when the door's basis is
  `anchor_measured`.
- If the range straddles the minimum, or the width is assumed rather than
  measured, say "cannot measure".
- Never say "compliant". ADA clear width is an accessibility rule, not an
  egress-capacity model; keep the two apart in the rationale.
