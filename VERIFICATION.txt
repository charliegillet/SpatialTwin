Coverage and limitations
Offline perception is explicitly injected only in automated tests. Tests cover
actual video decoding, invalid containers/length, provider normalization and
sanitized errors, finite dimensions, coordinate/yaw convention, polygon validity,
wall/opening attachments, stale revisions, persistence, calibration and held-out
residuals, robust versus inconclusive reachability, and provider failure without
fake success. Backend-only optional critic is not exposed as a completed UI flow.

No real user room recording was provided. This verifies integration with an actual
model call on synthetic footage; it does not establish real-room accuracy.
Single-anchor scale does not recover camera geometry or confirm inferred walls.
The held-out error demonstrates why manual correction and further measurements
remain necessary. Floor-supported upright boxes, one level room, and declared
uncertainty bounds are assumptions. Path statuses are conditional on the scene
and nominal calibrated scale; global measurement/camera errors are not propagated.
Layout search is bounded and tests final furniture poses, not globally minimal
solutions or swept physical movement. No kinematic wheelchair or safety
certification is claimed. Jobs are in memory; saved scenes and media survive
restarts, while in-flight jobs do not resume after a process restart.
